Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 August 2026. This policy explains what HiAir collects and why.

1. Who we are

HiAir is a wellness assistant for outdoor heat and air quality. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. Controller contact: hello@hiair.io.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: email and authentication identifiers for email, Sign in with Apple, or Google via Supabase
  • Profile: persona, optional date of birth (13+), language, and optional location when you allow it
  • Private wellness logs: symptom journal, risk scores, and recommendations, not shared with other users
  • Apple Health / Health Connect: only after you continue and grant system permission; daily/hourly aggregates, not raw sensor streams; HiAir does not write health data back
  • Purchases: App Store / Play transaction identifiers needed to unlock Premium
  • Device and push tokens if you enable notifications
  • Operational logs: path, status, latency, and request id; no advertising ID

3. Why we process data

  • Provide personalized outdoor-risk and wellness guidance
  • Deliver optional alerts you enable
  • Verify subscriptions and keep the service secure
  • Provide optional AI explanations of already-computed risk; no advertising or sale of data

4. Legal basis

  • Contract: account, core app features, and paid Premium
  • Consent: location, HealthKit / Health Connect, and optional wellness inputs
  • Legitimate interest: security, abuse prevention, and reliability

5. Sharing

We use processors that help run the service: Supabase (authentication/database), Cloudflare (hosting/API), Open-Meteo (weather/air quality), OpenAI (optional explanations), and Apple/Google for sign-in and store billing. We do not sell personal data, use it for third-party advertising, or track you across apps.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account-linked data while the account is active. In Settings you can export your data or delete your account. Deletion removes HiAir-stored personal data that we are not legally required to retain. Store subscriptions continue until you cancel them with Apple or Google.

7. Your rights

Depending on your region you may request access, correction, export, or deletion. Use the in-app export/delete controls or email hello@hiair.io.

8. Children

HiAir accounts are for users 13 and older. A “children outdoor” persona is guidance for an adult account holder, not a child account.
