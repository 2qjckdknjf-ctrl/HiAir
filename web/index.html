Science & Data

Clear inputs. Honest limits.

HiAir uses time-stamped weather and air-quality inputs from Open-Meteo where available, with recent cached results for resilience. The risk engine combines observed factors, profile context, and activity rules; unavailable data remains unavailable.

Read how HiAir guidance works →

Privacy

Specific controls, not vague promises.

HiAir may process your account email, optional coarse location, private wellness logs, and optional Apple Health aggregates. You can export or delete HiAir-stored data in the app. Personal data is not sold for advertising.
