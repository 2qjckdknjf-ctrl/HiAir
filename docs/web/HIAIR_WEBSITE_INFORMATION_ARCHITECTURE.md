# HiAir Website Information Architecture

Date: 2026-08-31

## Primary journey

| Order | Section | Visitor question | Primary proof/action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Header | Where can I learn or download? | Compact anchors, Science & Data route, Download |
| 2 | Hero | What does HiAir do? | Clear promise, real iOS dashboard, App Store badge |
| 3 | Availability | Is this a shipped product? | iOS version/platform status; truthful Android status |
| 4 | Comparison | Why not use a weather app? | Raw values versus a labeled HiAir example |
| 5 | Demo | What will the product tell me? | Deterministic city/activity example and store CTA |
| 6 | Product stories | How does it work? | Risk, timing, reasons, personalization |
| 7 | Apple Health | How can activity context help? | Optional data-flow explanation and boundary |
| 8 | Free/Premium | What is included? | Verified entitlement comparison; in-app pricing CTA |
| 9 | Science & Privacy | Why should I trust it? | Data limits, score boundary, specific privacy links |
| 10 | FAQ | What could block my decision? | Product, safety, factors, Apple Health, availability |
| 11 | Final CTA | What should I do next? | Official App Store badge and QR |
| 12 | Footer | Where are support/legal resources? | Science, guides, privacy, terms, contact |

## Navigation

- How it works → `/#how-it-works`
- Features → `/#features`
- Science & Data → `/methodology/`
- Premium → `/#premium`
- FAQ → `/#faq`
- Download → verified App Store listing

The existing audience and guide routes remain indexable and linked from the footer or related pages, but no longer compete with the main product journey in the header.

## Existing route map retained

- `/guides/` and three original guide articles
- `/for-families/`
- `/for-runners/`
- `/air-quality-sensitive/`
- `/methodology/` (presented as Science & Data)
- `/about/`, `/contact/`, `/privacy/`, `/terms/`
- `/404.html`, `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`

No thin keyword routes were added. The English-only site remains `lang=en`; hreflang is intentionally absent until a complete localized version exists.
